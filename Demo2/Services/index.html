Therapy That Fits
Your Life

A range of occupational therapy services designed to meet you where you are and support you where you want to be.

What's Available

Offered Services

Initial Assessment

Comprehensive · 90 min

A thorough evaluation of your occupational performance, functional abilities, goals, and environment. Includes standardised assessments, goal-setting, and a personalised treatment plan.

Functional ability evaluation
Goal identification & priority-setting
Written treatment plan provided

Individual Therapy Session

45 or 60 min

One-on-one therapy sessions targeting your specific goals. Sessions are structured, evidence-based, and progressively updated to reflect your achievements and changing needs.

Goal-directed skill training
Progress monitoring each session
Home programme provided

Home Visit Assessment

In-home · 90 min

Assessment conducted in your own home to evaluate safety, accessibility, and functional performance in your real environment. Includes home modification recommendations and equipment prescription.

Safety & fall risk evaluation
Home modification recommendations
Assistive equipment prescription

Hand Therapy & Splinting

Specialised · 60 min

Specialised therapy for hand, wrist, and upper limb injuries and conditions. Includes custom thermoplastic splinting and fabrication, scar management, and fine motor re-education.

Custom thermoplastic splinting
Fine motor re-education
Scar management & oedema control

Paediatric OT

Children · 45 min

Supporting children's developmental, sensory, and learning needs. Play-based therapy to build fine motor skills, sensory processing, self-care independence, and school readiness.

Sensory integration therapy
Fine motor & handwriting support
School & participation support

Workplace Assessment

Vocational · 90 min

Worksite assessments to evaluate ergonomics, injury risk, and capacity to participate in employment. Includes a detailed report with recommendations for workplace modifications and return-to-work plans.

Ergonomic workstation assessment
Return-to-work planning
Detailed written report
Transparent Pricing
