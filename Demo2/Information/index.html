Introduction

Hi, I'm Jessica Chen

I am a Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (BOT) graduate with over eight years of clinical experience working across acute, subacute, community, and paediatric settings. My journey into occupational therapy was driven by a deep belief that every person deserves to fully participate in the activities that give their life meaning.

Throughout my career, I have supported individuals recovering from stroke, traumatic brain injury, orthopaedic procedures and mental health challenges, as well as children navigating developmental and sensory processing difficulties. I bring both scientific rigour and compassionate listening to each therapeutic relationship.

My practice is grounded in evidence-based models including the Canadian Model of Occupational Performance and Engagement (CMOP-E) and the Person-Environment-Occupation (PEO) model, ensuring that therapy is always contextual, client-directed, and meaningful.
